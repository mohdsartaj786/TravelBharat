Rajasthan

Jaipur

The Pink City of India, famous for royal palaces, magnificent forts, colorful markets and rich Rajasthani culture.

About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. The city is famous for its royal architecture, historic forts, magnificent palaces, colorful bazaars, handicrafts and traditional cuisine. Jaipur is also an important part of India's Golden Triangle tourism circuit.

Top Places to Visit

Amber Fort

A magnificent hilltop fort known for its beautiful architecture, courtyards and royal history.

Hawa Mahal

The iconic Palace of Winds, famous for its distinctive windows and pink sandstone facade.

City Palace

A grand royal complex showcasing Jaipur's history, architecture and royal heritage.

Jantar Mantar

A historic astronomical observatory featuring impressive scientific instruments.

Nahargarh Fort

A beautiful hilltop fort offering panoramic views of Jaipur and its surrounding landscape.

Jal Mahal

A beautiful palace situated in the middle of Man Sagar Lake.

Things To Do

🏰 Explore Forts & Palaces

Discover Jaipur's magnificent forts, palaces and royal architecture.

🛍️ Shopping

Explore traditional markets for handicrafts, textiles, jewelry and Rajasthani souvenirs.

🍛 Try Rajasthani Food

Taste traditional dishes such as Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar and other local specialties.

📸 Photography

Capture the colorful streets, historic monuments, palaces and beautiful sunsets of Jaipur.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally considered a comfortable period to explore Jaipur. The weather is more pleasant for sightseeing and outdoor activities during these months.

How To Reach

✈️ By Air

Jaipur International Airport provides air connectivity with several major Indian cities.

🚆 By Train

Jaipur Junction is well connected with major cities across India by railway.

🚌 By Bus

Regular buses connect Jaipur with Delhi, Agra, Jodhpur, Udaipur and other cities.

🚗 By Road

Jaipur has good road connectivity with nearby cities and major tourist destinations.

Travel Tips

☀️ Protect Yourself From Sun

Carry sunglasses, sunscreen and a hat, especially when exploring outdoor monuments.

👟 Wear Comfortable Shoes

Comfortable footwear is recommended because many forts and monuments require walking.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Carry drinking water while exploring the city and its historic attractions.

🛍️ Bargain While Shopping

Bargaining may be common in some local markets, especially when buying handicrafts and souvenirs.
