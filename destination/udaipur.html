Rajasthan

Udaipur

The City of Lakes, famous for beautiful palaces, peaceful lakes, gardens and royal heritage.

About Udaipur

Udaipur is one of Rajasthan's most beautiful cities. Surrounded by the Aravalli Hills and several picturesque lakes, the city is known for its royal palaces, traditional architecture, colorful culture and scenic landscapes. Its peaceful atmosphere and historic charm make it one of India's most popular tourist destinations.

Top Places to Visit

City Palace

A magnificent royal palace overlooking Lake Pichola, known for its grand architecture and historic interiors.

Lake Pichola

A beautiful artificial lake surrounded by palaces, hills and scenic waterfront views.

Jag Mandir

A stunning island palace located on Lake Pichola, famous for its architecture and peaceful surroundings.

Saheliyon Ki Bari

A historic garden featuring fountains, marble structures, greenery and beautiful walking paths.

Fateh Sagar Lake

A peaceful lake surrounded by hills and popular for relaxing views and evening visits.

Monsoon Palace

A hilltop palace offering panoramic views of Udaipur, its lakes and surrounding Aravalli Hills.

Things To Do in Udaipur

🚤 Boat Ride

Enjoy a relaxing boat ride on Lake Pichola, especially during sunset.

🏰 Explore Palaces

Discover the history, architecture and royal heritage of Udaipur's magnificent palaces.

🌅 Sunset Views

Watch beautiful sunsets around Lake Pichola and Fateh Sagar Lake.

🍛 Try Rajasthani Food

Taste traditional dishes such as Dal Baati Churma, Gatte Ki Sabzi and other local specialties.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally the best and most pleasant time to visit Udaipur. The weather is comfortable for sightseeing, outdoor activities and exploring the lakes and palaces.

How To Reach Udaipur

✈️ By Air

Maharana Pratap Airport connects Udaipur with major Indian cities.

🚆 By Train

Udaipur railway station provides rail connectivity with several major cities across India.

🚌 By Road

Udaipur is well connected by highways and buses with cities across Rajasthan and nearby states.

🚗 Local Travel

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and local transport can be used to explore different parts of the city.

Travel Tips

☀️ Carry Sun Protection

Carry sunglasses, sunscreen and a water bottle while exploring the city.

👟 Wear Comfortable Shoes

Comfortable footwear is recommended for exploring palaces, markets and tourist attractions.

📸 Explore Local Markets

Visit local markets to experience traditional handicrafts, textiles and Rajasthani culture.

🌊 Plan Lake Visits

Evening hours are ideal for enjoying the lakes and sunset views.
