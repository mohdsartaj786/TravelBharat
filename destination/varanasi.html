Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the oldest living cities in the world and the spiritual heart of India.

About Varanasi

Varanasi, also known as Kashi and Banaras, is located on the banks of the sacred River Ganges. The city is famous for its ancient temples, ghats, spiritual traditions and beautiful Ganga Aarti.

Top Places to Visit

Dashashwamedh Ghat

Famous for its spectacular evening Ganga Aarti.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the most important Shiva temples in India.

Sarnath

A major Buddhist historical and spiritual destination.

Assi Ghat

A peaceful riverside ghat popular with visitors.

Manikarnika Ghat

One of the oldest and most significant ghats of Varanasi.

BHU

Banaras Hindu University is one of India's famous universities.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally a comfortable period to explore Varanasi.
