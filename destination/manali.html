Himachal Pradesh

Manali

A beautiful Himalayan destination surrounded by mountains, valleys, forests, rivers and snow-covered landscapes.

About Manali

Manali is a popular mountain destination in Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by the Himalayas, the town is known for beautiful valleys, rivers, forests, snow-covered mountains and adventure activities. Manali is an ideal destination for nature lovers, families, couples and adventure enthusiasts.

Top Places to Visit

Solang Valley

A popular valley known for adventure activities, beautiful mountain views and scenic landscapes.

Rohtang Pass

A famous high-altitude mountain pass known for snow, glaciers and breathtaking Himalayan scenery.

Hadimba Temple

An ancient wooden temple surrounded by beautiful cedar forests and peaceful natural surroundings.

Old Manali

A peaceful part of Manali famous for cafes, local shops, guesthouses and mountain views.

Vashisht Temple

A historic temple area known for traditional architecture and natural hot springs.

Beas River

A scenic Himalayan river flowing through the Manali valley and surrounded by beautiful landscapes.

Things To Do in Manali

🏔️ Mountain Sightseeing

Explore the beautiful Himalayan landscapes, valleys and snow-covered mountains.

🎿 Adventure Activities

Enjoy activities such as skiing, paragliding and other adventure experiences depending on season.

🌲 Nature Walks

Take peaceful walks through forests, valleys and scenic areas around Manali.

☕ Explore Old Manali

Visit local cafes, shops and peaceful streets while experiencing the relaxed mountain atmosphere.

Best Time to Visit

March to June is suitable for pleasant weather and outdoor activities. December to February attracts visitors who want to experience winter and snowfall. The ideal time depends on the activities you want to enjoy.

How To Reach Manali

✈️ By Air

The nearest major airport is Bhuntar Airport, from where Manali can be reached by road.

🚆 By Train

The nearest railway stations are connected to Manali by road transport.

🚌 By Bus

Regular buses connect Manali with cities such as Delhi, Chandigarh and other nearby destinations.

🚗 By Road

Manali is well connected by road and is a popular destination for scenic road trips.

Travel Tips

🧥 Carry Warm Clothes

Mountain weather can change quickly, so carry warm clothes, especially during winter.

👟 Wear Comfortable Shoes

Comfortable and suitable footwear is useful for walking and exploring mountain areas.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Carry drinking water while travelling around high-altitude and outdoor areas.

🌦️ Check Weather

Check local weather and road conditions before travelling to higher-altitude locations.
