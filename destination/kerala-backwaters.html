Kerala

Kerala Backwaters

Peaceful waterways, lush greenery, traditional houseboats and beautiful natural landscapes make Kerala's backwaters a unique travel experience.

About Kerala Backwaters

Kerala's backwaters are a network of lakes, canals, lagoons and waterways surrounded by coconut trees, villages and lush green landscapes. A traditional houseboat journey through these peaceful waterways is one of Kerala's most popular travel experiences.

Top Places to Visit

Alleppey

One of Kerala's most popular backwater destinations, famous for houseboats and scenic waterways.

Kumarakom

A peaceful backwater destination known for waterways, greenery and beautiful birdlife.

Houseboat Cruise

Experience Kerala's beautiful waterways while travelling on a traditional houseboat.

Vembanad Lake

One of the largest and most scenic lakes in Kerala, surrounded by beautiful natural landscapes.

Kuttanad

A unique region famous for waterways, villages and beautiful farming landscapes.

Pathiramanal Island

A beautiful island surrounded by Kerala's backwaters, known for its peaceful natural environment.

Things To Do

🚤 Houseboat Cruise

Enjoy a relaxing journey through Kerala's scenic canals, lakes and waterways.

🌴 Explore Villages

Experience traditional Kerala village life, coconut plantations and local surroundings.

📸 Photography

Capture beautiful landscapes, waterways, houseboats and peaceful village scenes.

🍛 Try Kerala Food

Enjoy traditional Kerala cuisine and fresh local dishes during your backwater journey.

Best Time to Visit

October to February is generally a comfortable period for enjoying Kerala's backwaters. The weather is pleasant for sightseeing, houseboat cruises and outdoor activities.

How To Reach

✈️ By Air

Cochin International Airport is one of the major airports used by travellers visiting Kerala's backwater destinations.

🚆 By Train

Kerala has good railway connectivity with major cities across India. Alleppey and nearby stations provide access to the backwater region.

🚌 By Bus

Regular buses connect major Kerala cities and towns with popular backwater destinations.

🚗 By Road

Roads connect Alleppey, Kumarakom and other backwater destinations with nearby cities.

Travel Tips

🧴 Carry Sun Protection

Carry sunscreen, sunglasses and other sun protection when spending time outdoors.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Keep drinking water with you during sightseeing and outdoor activities.

📷 Carry Your Camera

The backwaters offer beautiful scenery and plenty of opportunities for photography.

🌦️ Check Weather

Check the weather before planning outdoor activities and boat trips.
