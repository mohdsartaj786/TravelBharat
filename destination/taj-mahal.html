Uttar Pradesh • Agra

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of India's most iconic monuments and a timeless symbol of love.

About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a magnificent white marble monument located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. It is admired for its beautiful architecture, perfect symmetry, gardens, detailed marble craftsmanship and historical significance.

Top Places to Visit

Agra Fort

A historic Mughal fort located near the Taj Mahal, famous for its grand architecture and history.

Mehtab Bagh

A beautiful garden across the Yamuna River offering scenic views of the Taj Mahal.

Itmad-ud-Daulah

An elegant Mughal tomb often known as the Baby Taj because of its detailed marble work.

Fatehpur Sikri

A historic Mughal city near Agra known for its magnificent monuments and architecture.

Kinari Bazaar

A colorful traditional market where visitors can explore handicrafts, clothing and local products.

Yamuna River

Enjoy peaceful views of the Yamuna River and beautiful surroundings near the Taj Mahal.

Things To Do

📸 Photography

Capture the beautiful architecture, gardens and famous views of the Taj Mahal.

🏛️ Explore Mughal History

Visit Agra Fort and other historical monuments to learn about the Mughal era.

🌅 Sunrise Visit

Visit the Taj Mahal early in the morning to enjoy peaceful surroundings and beautiful light.

🛍️ Local Shopping

Explore Agra's markets for handicrafts, marble products, textiles and souvenirs.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally a comfortable season to explore Agra and visit the Taj Mahal. Morning and evening hours are especially pleasant for sightseeing.

How To Reach

✈️ By Air

Agra has an airport and is also accessible from nearby major airports such as Delhi.

🚆 By Train

Agra is well connected with major Indian cities through several railway stations.

🚌 By Road

Agra has good road connectivity with Delhi, Jaipur, Lucknow and other major cities.

🚗 Local Travel

Taxis, auto-rickshaws and local transport are available for exploring Agra.

Travel Tips

🎟️ Plan Your Visit

Plan your visit in advance and check monument timings before travelling.

👟 Comfortable Shoes

Wear comfortable footwear because sightseeing around Agra involves considerable walking.

💧 Carry Water

Keep a water bottle with you, especially during warmer months.

📷 Respect Rules

Follow monument guidelines and photography restrictions while visiting historical sites.
