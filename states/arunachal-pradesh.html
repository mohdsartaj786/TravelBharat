ABOUT THE STATE

India's easternmost frontier.

Arunachal Pradesh is the largest state in Northeast India, spanning deep Himalayan gorges, alpine meadows, and rich bio-reserves where the first rays of the Indian sun touch the earth.

From the magnificent heights of Tawang Monastery and the snowy switchbacks of Sela Pass to the cultural terraces of Ziro Valley and the wild biodiversity of Namdapha, Arunachal offers an unparallelled Himalayan expedition.

Explore the highlights.

High-altitude monastic towns, pristine tribal valleys, and mountain passes across Arunachal Pradesh.

01

Tawang

A high-altitude mountain town at 10,000 feet — home to the magnificent 400-year-old Tawang Monastery, scenic lakes, and war memorials.

02

Ziro Valley

A stunning UNESCO-shortlisted pine valley home to the Apatani tribe, famous for unique wet rice cultivation and the annual Ziro Music Festival.

03

Sela Pass

A majestic high-altitude mountain pass at 13,700 feet draped in snow year-round, flanked by the crystal-clear Paradise Lake.

04

Namdapha National Park

A pristine eastern Himalayan wilderness area harboring four wild feline species (tiger, leopard, snow leopard, and clouded leopard).

FOOD OF ARUNACHAL PRADESH

Taste the mountains.

Arunachali cuisine relies on wholesome organic ingredients, smoked meats, wild forest bamboo shoots, boiled herbs, and indigenous rice beer.

Thukpa A comforting, hot Monpa noodle soup simmered with fresh mountain vegetables, garlic, and shredded meat.
Zan A nourishing and spicy millet or maize porridge often paired with charred pork or local herbs.
Pika Pila A famous fiery pickle prepared by the Apatani tribe using tender bamboo shoots, pork fat, and king chili.
Apong A traditional fermented rice beer brewed at home, served ceremoniously to welcome guests during festivals.
CULTURE

The soul of Arunachal Pradesh.

01

26 Major Tribes

A vibrant mosaic of indigenous communities including Monpa, Adi, Nyishi, and Mishmi preserving distinct oral histories and attire.

02

Losar Festival

The joyous Tibetan Buddhist New Year celebrated across Tawang with masked monastic dances, spiritual prayers, and family feasts.

03

Bamboo & Cane Craft

Masterful artisan traditions crafting intricate suspension bridges, headgears, baskets, and ceremonial ornaments from cane.

04

Mahayana Buddhism

Rich monastic traditions in the western districts reflected in colorful Thangka cloth paintings, prayer wheels, and stone mani walls.
