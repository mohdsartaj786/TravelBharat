ABOUT THE STATE

A paradise of nature & serenity.

Kerala is a lush tropical strip nestled along the Malabar Coast between the Arabian Sea and the mist-laden Western Ghats, renowned for eco-tourism, organic spice hills, and timeless waterways.

From gliding on luxury houseboats through the interconnected canals of Alleppey and walking through the colonial spice alleys of Fort Kochi to hiking in the tea hills of Munnar and exploring the rainforests of Wayanad, Kerala offers a restorative escape.

Explore the highlights.

Celebrated backwater lagoons, hill stations, and heritage port towns in Kerala.

01

Munnar

Queen of South Indian hill stations — famous for rolling tea carpet slopes, Anamudi peak, Eravikulam Nilgiri Tahr sanctuary, and mist-filled valleys.

02

Alleppey (Alappuzha)

Venice of the East — renowned for traditional Kettuvallam houseboat cruises along Vembanad Lake, paddy fields, and coir villages.

03

Kochi

Queen of the Arabian Sea — celebrated for iconic Chinese fishing nets, Portuguese Mattancherry Palace, Jew Town, and art cafes.

04

Wayanad

An idyllic high-altitude plateau featuring prehistoric petroglyphs at Edakkal Caves, bamboo rainforests, and spice plantations.

FOOD OF KERALA

Taste the tradition.

Kerala cuisine is an aromatic celebration of freshly grated coconut, cold-pressed coconut oil, curry leaves, mustard seeds, and Malabar black pepper.

Kerala Sadya A grand vegetarian banquet featuring 24+ dishes served on plantain leaves, complete with Avial, Sambar, and Payasam.
Appam with Stew Lacy, soft fermented rice-coconut hoppers paired with a gentle, fragrant vegetable or chicken coconut milk stew.
Meen Pollichathu & Karimeen Pearl spot fish marinated in spicy shallot-masala, wrapped in banana leaves and slow pan-roasted.
Puttu & Kadala Curry Steamed cylindrical rice-coconut cakes served alongside spicy black chickpea curry tempered with roasted coconut shreds.
CULTURE

The soul of Kerala.

01

Kathakali Dance-Drama

Centuries-old classical dance drama featuring elaborate stylized green makeup (Paccha), monumental headgear, and expressive eye gestures.

02

Theyyam Ritual Art

An ancient North Malabar ritual shamanic dance where performers personify divine deities in towering bamboo headdresses and body paint.

03

Onam & Boat Races

Grand harvest festival marked by floral carpets (Pookkalam), Pulikali tiger dances, and the thunderous Nehru Trophy Snake Boat Races (Vallam Kali).

04

Ayurveda & Kalaripayattu

Holistic Ayurvedic healing therapies and Kalaripayattu, one of the world's most ancient mother martial art systems.
