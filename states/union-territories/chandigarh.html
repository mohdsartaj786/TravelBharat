ABOUT THE UNION TERRITORY

A city of design & harmony.

Chandigarh is India's first planned city, serving jointly as the vibrant capital of both Punjab and Haryana while holding independent Union Territory status.

Designed masterfully by Swiss-French architect Le Corbusier, Chandigarh is celebrated globally for its grid-iron sector layout, abundant green lungs, wide boulevards, and high quality of urban life.

MUST VISIT

Explore the highlights.

Iconic sculpture parks, man-made lakes, rose gardens, and UNESCO architectural complexes.

01

Rock Garden

A sprawling 40-acre sculpture labyrinth entirely built from industrial waste, discarded ceramics, bangles, and home rubble by creator Nek Chand.

02

Sukhna Lake

A serene 3-km rain-fed lake at the foothills of the Shivalik hills, popular for boating, sunset strolls, and migratory bird watching.

03

Zakir Hussain Rose Garden

Asia's largest botanical rose garden spanning 30 acres, featuring over 1,600 species of vibrant roses and annual spring flower festivals.

04

Capitol Complex

A UNESCO World Heritage architectural site comprising the High Court, Secretariat, and Legislative Assembly designed by Le Corbusier.

FOOD OF CHANDIGARH

Taste the Punjabi zest.

Chandigarh’s culinary scene is a vibrant extension of Punjabi hospitality — celebrated for buttery dhaba flavors, rich dairy, tandoori grills, and street food hubs.

Chole Bhature & Kulche Spicy chickpea masala served with deep-fried puffy bhature or tandoor-baked stuffed Amritsari kulche drenched in butter.
Butter Chicken & Dal Makhani Velvety tomato-butter gravies slow-cooked with cream and shredded chicken or whole black lentils overnight.
Makki di Roti & Sarson da Saag Traditional winter comfort food made of yellow corn flatbreads paired with mustard greens and melted white butter.
Patiala Lassi Thick, creamy churned yogurt topped with a heavy layer of fresh malai and chopped dry fruits in tall brass glasses.
CULTURE

The soul of Chandigarh.

01

Modernist Urban Planning

Pioneering the concept of urban sectors (modeled on human body systems) that revolutionized post-independence Indian city design.

02

Vibrant Punjabi Lifestyle

An infectious zest for life reflected in bhangra music, high-fashion street markets in Sector 17, and lively open-air terrace dining.

03

Art & Sculpture Heritage

A thriving community of artists nurtured by the Government Museum and Art Gallery and Nek Chand's outsider art legacy.

04

Green City Eco-Ethos

A disciplined urban environment where clean tree-lined promenades, bicycle tracks, and community parks foster absolute serenity.
