ABOUT THE UNION TERRITORY

A world of coral & azure.

Lakshadweep, meaning "A Hundred Thousand Islands" in Sanskrit, is India's smallest Union Territory, comprising 36 exotic coral islands scattered across the sunlit Arabian Sea.

From the bustling local life in Kavaratti and the breathtaking aerial approaches of Agatti to the secluded, bioluminescent shores of Bangaram, Lakshadweep is the ultimate destination for scuba diving, lagoon kayaking, and tranquil isolation.

MUST VISIT

Explore the islands.

Exotic coral atolls, marine sanctuaries, and tranquil beaches in Lakshadweep.

01

Kavaratti

The administrative capital known for its ornate Ujra Mosque carved from driftwood, marine aquarium, and excellent lagoon scuba diving.

02

Agatti Island

The breathtaking gateway featuring India's most scenic airstrip, surrounded by vivid blue lagoons, coral beds, and water sports.

03

Bangaram Island

A stunning, teardrop-shaped uninhabited island accessible from Agatti, famous for bioluminescent plankton and serene tropical isolation.

04

Kalpeni

Renowned for its massive storm bank of coral debris along the eastern shoreline, shallow wading lagoons, and the iconic Tipam Reef.

FOOD OF LAKSHADWEEP

Taste the ocean.

Lakshadweep cuisine is heavily influenced by the Malabar coast, relying extensively on the ocean's bounty, freshly harvested coconuts, and locally grown rice.

Mus Kavaab A signature island delicacy of spicy, thick tuna curry slow-cooked with coconut milk, coriander, and curry leaves.
Octopus Fry Crispy, deep-fried tender baby octopus marinated in a fiery blend of red chilies, garlic, and sea salt.
Coconut Rice & Mass Min Fragrant rice cooked in fresh coconut milk, typically served alongside sun-dried, smoked tuna (Mass Min).
Kadalakka & Banana Halwa Traditional sweet treats made of sugar-coated chickpeas (Kadalakka) and rich, dark caramelized banana halwa.
CULTURE

The soul of Lakshadweep.

01

Marine Heritage

An eco-sensitive way of life where the ocean is sacred, relying on sustainable pole-and-line tuna fishing and coral preservation.

02

Lava Dance

A vibrant, rhythmic traditional folk dance originally from Minicoy island, performed by men in colorful costumes and drum beats.

03

Matrilineal Society

Unique among Islamic communities globally, Minicoy islanders follow a matrilineal descent system influenced by ancient Malabar customs.

04

Coir & Driftwood Craft

Artisanal heritage centered around the coconut tree, producing fine coir ropes, woven mats, and intricate driftwood carvings.
