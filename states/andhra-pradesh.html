ABOUT THE STATE

A land of devotion & destiny.

Andhra Pradesh is a captivating southeastern state stretching along the Bay of Bengal, cradled by the Eastern Ghats and renowned for ancient dynasties, Buddhist heritage, and vibrant arts.

From the holy hills of Tirupati and the coastal beauty of Visakhapatnam to the coffee plantations of Araku Valley and the ancient Buddhist stupas of Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh offers a rich cultural journey.

Explore the highlights.

Sacred temple towns, scenic hill stations, and coastal harbors across Andhra Pradesh.

01

Tirupati

The spiritual capital — home to the world-renowned Sri Venkateswara Temple atop the Tirumala hills, drawing millions of devoted pilgrims.

02

Visakhapatnam (Vizag)

The City of Destiny — featuring scenic beaches like Rushikonda, Kailasagiri hill park, and the historic INS Kurusura Submarine Museum.

03

Araku Valley

An enchanting hill station in the Eastern Ghats, famous for organic coffee plantations, cascading waterfalls, and the million-year-old Borra Caves.

04

Amaravati & Vijayawada

Ancient seats of Buddhist art and historic Krishna riverfront temples, anchored by the iconic Kanaka Durga Temple in Vijayawada.

FOOD OF ANDHRA

Taste the fire.

Andhra cuisine is celebrated for its rich, fiery, and tangy flavors, making generous use of red chilies, tamarind, sesame, and native spices.

Gongura Pachadi A signature tangy and spicy chutney made from red sorrel leaves, considered an absolute staple of every traditional Andhra meal.
Pesarattu A nutritious, crispy green gram (moong dal) crepe, frequently served stuffed with savory potato upma and ginger chutney.
Natu Kodi Pulusu A robust, fiery country chicken curry slow-cooked with roasted spices, onions, and tamarind extract.
Pootharekulu A delicate, paper-thin sweet pastry originating from Atreyapuram, made with starch sheets, ghee, powdered sugar, and nuts.
CULTURE

The soul of Andhra.

01

Kuchipudi Classical Dance

Originating from Krishna district, this world-renowned classical dance style blends fast rhythmic footwork with expressive theatrical storytelling.

02

Kalamkari Textile Art

An ancient hand-painting and block-printing craft on cotton textiles using organic vegetable dyes, depicting grand mythological epics.

03

Kondapalli Toys

Exquisite lightweight wooden toys hand-carved by master artisans in Kondapalli village, depicting rural village scenes and mythology.

04

Ugadi & Sankranti

Major festive celebrations marking the Telugu New Year (Ugadi) with Ugadi Pachadi and multi-day harvest festivities of Sankranti.
