ABOUT THE STATE

A land of hills & harmony.

Mizoram is a peaceful northeastern state nestled amidst verdant mountain ranges, celebrated for its high literacy, warm community ethos (Tlawmngaihna), and untouched nature.

From the bustling clifftop capital of Aizawl and the thunderous cascades of Vantawng Falls to the highest peak at Phawngpui and the traditional heritage village of Reiek, Mizoram offers a rejuvenating escape into pristine mountain tranquility.

MUST VISIT

Explore the highlights.

Picturesque valleys, mountain summits, and iconic waterfalls of Mizoram.

01

Aizawl

The ridge-top capital city — famous for Solomon's Temple, Durtlang Hills, Mizoram State Museum, and Bara Bazar handlooms.

02

Vantawng Falls

The highest two-tiered waterfall in Mizoram, plunging 750 feet amidst dense bamboo groves and evergreen rainforests.

03

Phawngpui

The Blue Mountain — Mizoram's highest peak at 2,157 meters, renowned for rare orchids, rhododendrons, and cliffside panoramas.

04

Reiek

A scenic mountain peak and model Mizo heritage village showcasing traditional bamboo huts, tribal life, and trekking paths.

FOOD OF MIZORAM

Taste the tradition.

Mizo cuisine is wholesome and mild — characterized by organic wild hill herbs, smoked meats, steamed vegetables, and nutritious bamboo shoots with minimal oil.

Bai A popular staple stew cooked with seasonal green leaves, bamboo shoots, fermented pork, and cooking soda.
Vawksa Rep Smoked pork roasted over wood fire and stir-fried with mustard leaves, ginger, and local green chilies.
Misa Mach Poora Succulent river shrimp grilled on skewers inside banana leaves, seasoned with roasted mustard and lime.
Sawhchiar A comforting traditional porridge cooked with indigenous rice, tender chicken, bay leaves, and subtle seasonings.
CULTURE

The soul of Mizoram.

01

Cheraw Bamboo Dance

Mizoram's most iconic dance where performers step rhythmically between pairs of clapped bamboo poles in vibrant Puan attire.

02

Chapchar Kut

The joyous spring festival celebrating the completion of Jhum clearing with music, feasts, colorful costumes, and community dances.

03

Puan Handloom Weaving

Intricate, colorful handwoven traditional shawls and skirts (Puanchei, Ngotekherh) woven with pride on backstrap looms.

04

Cane & Bamboo Art

Expert local craft producing conical hats (Khumbeu), woven storage baskets, furniture, and sustainable everyday utensils.
