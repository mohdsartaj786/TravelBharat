Assam

Discover the gateway to Northeast India — home to sprawling emerald tea estates, the legendary one-horned rhinoceros in Kaziranga, the world's largest river island of Majuli, and vibrant Bihu heritage.

ABOUT THE STATE

A land of blue hills & red rivers.

Assam is a breathtaking northeastern sanctuary bound by the mighty Brahmaputra river, celebrated worldwide for its aromatic black tea, rich wildlife biodiversity, and ancient Vaishnavite monasteries.

From tracking rhinos through the tall grasslands of Kaziranga and experiencing the tranquil spiritual island of Majuli to paying respects at the historic Kamakhya Temple in Guwahati, Assam offers an unforgettable cultural voyage.

MUST VISIT

Explore the highlights.

UNESCO wildlife reserves, massive river islands, and ancient Ahom kingdom capitals.

01

Kaziranga National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — home to two-thirds of the world's great one-horned rhinoceros population, wild water buffaloes, and tigers.

02

Majuli

The world's largest river island on the Brahmaputra — renowned for traditional neo-Vaishnavite Satras, mask-making crafts, and serene wetlands.

03

Guwahati

The bustling gateway metropolis — famous for the hilltop Kamakhya Temple, Peacock Island river cruises, and vibrant silk markets.

04

Sivasagar

The ancient royal capital of the Ahom Dynasty — featuring magnificent stone palaces, royal amphitheatres, and massive ancient water tanks.

FOOD OF ASSAM

Taste the flavors.

Assamese cuisine is distinct for its subtle, non-spicy flavors, featuring fresh endemic river fish, fermented bamboo shoots, and traditional alkaline broths.

Masor Tenga A signature tangy and refreshing light fish soup soured with elephant apple (ou tenga) or tomatoes and lime.
Assamese Khar A unique traditional digestive preparation made by filtering water through sun-dried banana trunk ashes, cooked with papaya or lentils.
Duck Meat with Kumura A celebratory delicacy prepared with succulent duck meat slow-cooked with tender ash gourd and whole aromatic spices.
Bihu Pitha & Joha Rice Traditional sweet rice cakes (Sunga pitha, Bhodia pitha) paired with aromatic scented Joha rice and fresh buffalo curd.
CULTURE

The soul of Assam.

01

Bihu Festival

The vibrant tri-annual festival cycle (Bohag, Kati, Magh Bihu) celebrating agricultural seasons with energetic Bihu dance and Dhol beats.

02

Golden Muga Silk

Assam's exclusive natural golden silk (GI-tagged), traditionally handwoven into elegant Mekhela Sador garments worn during festivals.

03

Sattriya Classical Dance

A graceful classical Indian dance form rooted in the 15th-century monastic Satras of Majuli, portraying mythological narratives.

04

Assam Tea Heritage

Centuries-old sprawling colonial tea estates producing the world's most robust malty black teas, integral to Assamese identity.
