1. Overview

Welcome to TravelBharat. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of everyone who visits our platform to discover India's culture, food, destinations, and state heritage.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed, and your rights concerning your personal information when using our website.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to enhance your exploration and user experience:

  • Contact Details: When you send inquiries via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Favorites & Saved Preferences: Destination bookmarks and theme settings saved directly in your browser.
  • Technical Analytics: Device type, browser version, approximate geography, and pages viewed to improve website navigation and responsiveness.

3. How We Use Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Respond to your questions, feedback, and travel queries.
  • Personalize and preserve your favorite travel destinations.
  • Optimize platform responsiveness across desktop and mobile devices.
  • Prevent malicious activity, spam submissions, and technical abuse.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

TravelBharat utilizes modern browser LocalStorage instead of intrusive tracking cookies to store your saved bucket lists (favorites) and theme mode preferences. This data remains on your local machine and is never sold to advertising brokers.

5. Data Protection & Security

We deploy standard SSL encryption protocols to ensure that all data exchanged between your web browser and our servers remains private, encrypted, and secure.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external websites, official state tourism portals, or map navigation services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of messages submitted through our contact channels, and you can clear your saved favorites at any time directly through your browser settings.

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data inquiries, please reach out to us:
